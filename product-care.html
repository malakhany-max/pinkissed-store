Tips & Tricks

Product Care

How to Keep Your Pins Happy & Shiny!

💧

Keep Them Dry

Water and pins don't mix! Keep them away from rain, pools, and accidental trips to the washing machine.

📦

Store Them Like Treasure

Keep them in a jewelry box or soft pouch so they don't get scratched or lost.

✨

No Perfume, Please

Avoid direct contact with perfume, creams, lotion, or sprays to keep your accessories shiny and beautiful.

❤️

Be Gentle With Them

Handle your pins and accessories softly to keep them secure and looking their best.

⏰

Last On, First Off

Put your accessories on after perfume and makeup, then remove them first at the end of the day.

💎

Keep Them Clean

A soft cloth is all you need to wipe away dust or fingerprints. No soap needed!

🔒

Secure That Backing

Check pin backs often to make sure they're snug so they don't escape!

🛍️

Handle With Love

Avoid dropping, bending, or using them as ninja stars. They need gentle care!
